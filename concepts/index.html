Twelve ways to get insulted by your own game

Rough concepts only, nothing is planned in depth yet. Each one has two mock-up screens (drawn, not playable), the one feature that makes it different, and the goal. Mark each Yes, Maybe or No, jot a note if you want to steal a part of it for another idea, then hit Copy my picks and paste the result back to Claude. Click any screen to enlarge it. Your marks are saved in this browser.

Core = tower defense plus automated production, as in the original idea. Wild = stretches the brief but still fits 1 to 6 players, one friend hosting, and specialist roles. Big map = the core idea on a large isometric map that each player scrolls around, with production and defense in different places.
