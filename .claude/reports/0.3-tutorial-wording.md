# 0.3.03 training shift wording

Every lesson of the training shift as the panel shows it now, on a desktop and on a touch screen, with what DEB says when the lesson starts. Made from the code (src/tutorial/lessons.js; a touch screen shows a lesson's own touch words where it has them, else its desktop words, run through src/content/touch.js either way). On a phone the panel shows the "Do this:" lines and keeps the rest behind its More button.

## What changed (release 0.3.03, the owner's request: "The training shift should only teach about a few different shoppers, just to show that there's variation. It should not show all shoppers. It should show all buildings though.")

- **Shoppers sent in:** only Coupon Clippers (super ones, Extreme Couponers, in the upgrade lesson), Dads and one Gym Bro. Bargain Hunters left the Wet Floor, Free Samples and upgrade lessons.
- **Lessons removed (5):** Teenagers; Karens; Shoppers you cannot slow down (Man with Guide Dog, Mobility Scooter); Mom and Kid; Finish her while the kid is in the cart.
- **Lessons added (2):** Hold them up: the TV Salesman; Aim down the line: the Barcode Laser.
- **Lessons changed:** Stopping dads also asks for a Crate (so every dad crosses the belt on one row); the upgrade lesson moved before part 2; Part 2's introduction, Wet Floor, Free Samples and Training complete were reworded.
- **Lessons now:** 20 (was 23). Part 2 ("Jump to part 2") starts at lesson 15.

**Desktop:**

> Do this: read this, then press Next. Every lesson starts with a line like this one, and the big yellow arrow points at what to press.

> You have two jobs. Build a factory that earns cash. Build security that wears shoppers down until they give up and leave.
>

## 2. Start a production line: the Unpacker

**DEB:** Unpacker on the highlighted tile, arrow pointing down. It makes the boxes that everything else turns into money.

**Desktop:**

> Do this: click Unpacker in the bar at the bottom (or press 2), press R until the arrow on the preview points DOWN, then click the highlighted tile. Every production line starts with an Unpacker: it makes a Mystery Box every 2.5 seconds, for free.
>
> It pushes each box out of the side its arrow points to, so the arrow decides which way your line runs.
>
> The painted, named patches of floor (LOADING BAY, PRESS SHOP, GIFT WRAP, SHIPPING) are where the four factory machines go, each only on its own: Unpackers on Loading Bay. The patches are apart, so goods travel between them on belts. Belts, crates and security can go anywhere.

**Touch:**

> Do this: drag Unpacker from the bar at the bottom onto the highlighted tile. It lands facing right: tap it, then tap ⟳ Rotate on its card until its arrow points DOWN.
>
> While you drag, the building rides above your finger so you can see where it lands. (Or tap its button, then tap the tile; then tap DROP to put it down before you tap the building.) ## 3. Belts carry goods

**DEB:** Belt from the Unpacker down to the last highlighted tile, in one drag. Belts face the way you drag them.

**Desktop:**

> Do this: click Belt (or press 1), then press the mouse button on the first highlighted tile, drag straight down to the last one, and let go. Belts carry goods the way their arrows point, and they point the way you drag.
>
> Then watch the boxes ride down and pile up at the end: goods wait whenever the next tile cannot take them.

**Touch:**

> Then watch the boxes ride down and pile up at the end: goods wait whenever the next tile cannot take them.
