## What was done, most important first

### The factory is a second floor, "The Back", and it makes supplies instead of money
- Tab (or the button at the bottom right) switches floors. The floor you are not on shows through faintly.
- Fixed **Returns Drops** put out returned junk. Five machines turn junk into supplies: **Bottled Spite** (damage), **Rage Cola** (speed), **Extension Cord** (range), **Duct Tape** (soaks gym bro hits), **Razor Wire** (stops dads).
- The **Rebrander** raises a supply one brand tier (Store Brand, Name Brand, Premium, Platinum, worth the old upgrade levels 2, 4, 6, 8), slower at each step.
- **Belts, Splitters, Overpasses and Unpaid Interns** carry supplies to the **Dumbwaiter** under each security building. A stocked building is stronger; stock is used per shot; an empty building is exactly the old un-upgraded one. Leftovers go to a **Clearance Bin** for pennies.
- The Splitter shares evenly between its connected outputs and passes over one that is backed up, as you specified; tests pin the slow-output and recovery cases.
- Bought upgrades, the old shop-floor belts and machines, goods, the Shipping Chute and the marked floors are gone from the rules, the interface, the words, the sounds and the tests.

### Dads walk through everything; Razor Wire stops them
- Any security building can be given a wire direction (Wire tool, key Z, or its sheet). The wire covers the building and 3 tiles that way, and works only while the building holds Razor Wire. The tier is how many dads one unit turns away (3, 6, 10, 15).
- A wire plan that would cut dads off from the tills is refused. Two routes are drawn: "DADS" and "DADS, IF WIRED".

### Money comes from winning
- Event pay is $160 + $40 per event on Normal, plus a speed bonus of up to half of it that drains from the moment the last shopper is in, scaled by the share of shoppers stopped. Opening the doors early no longer pays anything. Thieves steal stock from buildings, not goods from belts.

### Crate Stacks can be reinforced for cash
- Reinforce tool, key U: eight levels at $10, 16, 26, 41, 66, 105, 168, 268 (the 0.3.04 crate durability prices), one more gym bro hit per event each.

### Your interface list
- Reviews go 25% sooner, close on a click or tap, and have a "Hide reviews" control. DEB sits at the bottom on a phone (she had been at the top right on narrow screens since 0.1.07, and an invisible box of hers was swallowing taps). The placement card has "don't show these", with a double click to show one. The three PC panels always stack and fold. The building sheet on a phone is compact.
- A new check, `node tools/layout.mjs`, measures how much of the floor the interface covers at four screen sizes and fails if limits are exceeded. On an 844x390 phone: build pause 27% to 24%, sheet open 84% to 36%, training 71% to about 32%.
- Shopper routes are always drawn.

### The stutter you saw around events 11 to 16
- Measured on 0.3.03: not processor load. Ranked causes: late or lost packets freezing the joined player (about 15 short freezes a minute on a typical mobile link); shopper pictures painted the first time they were needed, which peaked in events 11 to 16; a full copy of the game sent every 10 seconds on a slow uplink; the host's tab in the background.
- Fixed: the joined player's buffer adapts (15 freezes a minute to about 2 on the model of a mobile link, at about 0.13 s more delay); full copies once a minute; pictures painted in the build pause (1,561 painted during events 10 to 16 before, 376 after) and kept across zoom steps (worst zoom frame on a simulated phone 121 ms to 16.5 ms); a notice when the host tabs away.
- Limits: a model of the network and a slowed browser, not a real phone or link. Full write-up: `.claude/reports/2026-11-26-stutter-investigation.md`.

### Training, Handbook, DEB, sounds
- The training shift has 22 lessons (about 12 to 15 minutes) and teaches The Back, supplies, running dry, the Rebrander, Razor Wire, reinforcing and money. Each lesson re-marks anything the player removed or turned that it depends on. The Overpass and Clearance Bin are only described.
- The Handbook opens on a "How it works" page. DEB has 111 new lines for the new moments. Nine new sounds.

### A security hole from before 0.4 is closed
- A tampered browser could send a "place" command with the kind `__proto__`, which the host accepted, turning that player's cash into not-a-number. It was in 0.3.04. Every command that takes a name from a client is now guarded and tested.
