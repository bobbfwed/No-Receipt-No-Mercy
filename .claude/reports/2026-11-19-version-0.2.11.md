## What was done

### Survival of the scripted players (Normal, one player, Open Warehouse, eight games each)
The number is the sales event each scripted player loses in, averaged over eight seeds. "Honest" builds a sound layout and stops spending; "keen" keeps buying and upgrades everything; "focus" pours upgrades into the sprinklers nearest the entrance.

| **0.2.11 now** | **9.0** | **11.4** | **11.6** | **11 of 15** | 8.8 |

- ⚠️ **Three games still collapse early.** All three players lose in event 6 or 7, with 14 or 15 of the 20 refunds gone by the end of event 5. The extra dads in events 2 to 4 get through, each refund takes double cash, and the player cannot afford to build.
- **One game that collapsed before now survives** to event 10 to 13. The smaller event 5 and the slower boss were enough.
- **The other four games are about as hard as 0.2.10:** the upgrading players lose in event 14 to 16, as before.
- **The boss is beaten much more often:** 11 of 15 visits for the upgrading player, against 10 of 19 in 0.2.10.
- **The quieter late game shows only on Easy,** where the scripted players get far enough. Endless games, same eight seeds, 0.2.10 to now: honest 12.0 to 12.0, keen 17.9 to 18.4, focus 16.8 to 17.4.
- **How far to trust this:** the scripted players follow one fixed layout and a person adapts. The weak spot they show (early leaks snowballing against a limit of 20) is the same one the halved crowd showed in 0.2.09.
