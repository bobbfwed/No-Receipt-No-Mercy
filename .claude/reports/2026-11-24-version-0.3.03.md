# Version 0.3.03: phone dragging, a clearer training shift, volume sliders, income panel (2026-11-24)

## Needs your attention

- **Nothing is pushed.** `main` is ahead of GitHub by this release only (0.3.03; the release number went up once). Push when you are ready.
- **Everything on a phone was checked by emulation**, with simulated fingers in a desktop browser at 390 x 844. A real phone still has to confirm three things: that an upward drag on the bar picks a building up while a sideways swipe scrolls the bar; how the lift above the finger feels; and the training arrow's motion.
- **A held building's card now starts folded on a phone in every game**, not only in training. It was covering the tile a lesson wanted tapped. A player who unfolds it keeps that choice.
- **At 1280 x 720 with one player the INCOME panel sits beside the HAND strip, not below it**, because directly below would cover the left entrance. At 1920 x 1080 it is in the column as you asked.
- **The INCOME figure shows a dash for the first 10 seconds** of a game or after a reload, until it has history.
- **Sounds were measured, not heard.** The sliders were checked by rendering sounds and comparing levels.
- **The training shift still does not teach:** the cards (it has none), moving the view, buying Range, Speed or Durability, and turning a building after it is built (only through its card). No lessons were added; say if you want them.

## What was built, most important first

### 1. On a phone a building can be dragged from the bottom bar onto the floor
- Press a building's button and drag up: a preview follows, about one and a half tiles above your finger, joined to it by a thin line; lifting places one building where the preview is.
- **Your rule for taps:** a tap with a building held places it exactly where you tapped, however long the finger rests. Moving the finger more than 8 pixels lifts the preview, and it stays lifted for that touch.
- A sideways swipe on the bar still scrolls it. Tapping a button to hold a building still works. A drag does not leave the building held.
- Holding within 48 pixels of the floor's edge slides the view, up to 8 tiles a second.
- Letting go with the preview off the floor or over the bar builds nothing. A second finger still cancels; the Rotate button still shows.
- The belt dragged from the bar places one belt; a line of belts is still tap, then drag on the floor, drawn under the finger.
- Pictures: [dragging from the bar](img/0.3-bardrag-from-bar.png), [a refused spot](img/0.3-bardrag-refused.png), [a two-tile building](img/0.3-bardrag-two-tiles.png), [after an edge slide](img/0.3-bardrag-edge-slide.png).

### 2. The training shift points at what to press and says it plainly
- **Arrow:** a big yellow arrow bounces on the next thing to press and moves on as each is done: a bar button, the marked tile, OPEN THE DOORS, Next, the Damage button on a building's card. An ordinary game never shows it. [Desktop](img/0.3-arrow-desk-button.png), [on a tile](img/0.3-arrow-desk-tile.png).
- **Drag cue on a phone:** a finger dot travels from the bar button to the marked tile, with a ghost of the building where it will land. [Picture](img/0.3-arrowfix-3-cue.png).
- **Wording:** every lesson opens with a highlighted "Do this:" sentence. DEB says what to do with at most one short joke, and none in the seven lessons that introduce a control. On a touch screen the placing lessons teach dragging from the bar first, with "or tap its button, then tap the tile" as the other way. Every lesson before and after: [0.3-tutorial-wording.md](0.3-tutorial-wording.md).
- A test plays the whole shift on desktop and touch and fails if any lesson has no arrow or points at something that is not there.

### 3. ⚠️ Trying the drag and the arrow together found nine phone problems; all are fixed
- The serious one: a held building's card covered the marked tile, so a tap on the tile hit the card and built nothing. The card now starts folded on a phone. [Picture](img/0.3-arrowfix-1-chute-held.png).
- The drag cue no longer plays during a real drag, and its line passes under DEB's bubble.
- The arrow for DROP points at DROP. On a building's card, Rotate and Remove are readable at phone width. [Picture](img/0.3-arrowfix-5-card.png).
- The lesson panel on a phone is about a third of the screen: the title and "Do this:", the rest behind a More button.
- One small flaw is left: with a long hint showing, a sliver of the More button shows under it.

### 4. Volume sliders for each kind of sound
- Behind the speaker button: Volume (master), then Money, Shoppers, Security and machines, Announcements, Store music, DEB's voice. Each has a speaker mark that switches it off and back to its last level; letting go of a slider plays a sample; Reset restores the defaults. [Picture](img/0.3-volumes-2-desktop-two-muted.png), [phone](img/0.3-volumes-3-phone.png).
- With default settings every sound comes out as before (ten sounds rendered before and after matched to five decimal places). Old on/off settings carry over.
- Judgment calls you may want changed: a refund paid out is in Money; babies and puppies reaching a till are in Shoppers; the boss leaving is Shoppers and his arrival Announcements; the TV Salesman's patter is Security and machines.

### 5. INCOME panel
- The whole store's income from goods the factory sold over the last 60 seconds of game time, as dollars a minute, with "split 3 ways: $350 / min each" when several play. Only factory sales count; pausing and the speed buttons do not change it.
- Checked against the game's own totals on the demo layout: $1,081 measured against $1,080 shown; worst case within 3%.
- On a phone it is a "+$1,240/min" chip beside the cards; tapping it opens a card with the split and what is counted. [1920 wide](img/0.3-income-1920-cards.png), [three players](img/0.3-income-1280-3players.png), [phone card open](img/0.3-income-phone-390-card.png).

### 6. Your question about locking the screen (no changes made)
- Android Chrome can hold portrait or landscape, but only while the game is in full screen. iPhone Safari very likely cannot. A "turn your phone" screen works everywhere. The phone layout was built upright, so locking to landscape would need a landscape layout first.

## How to verify
1. `node --test`.
2. `node tools/serve.mjs`, then `http://localhost:5173/?tutorial=1` for the training shift; `/?demo=1` for a running factory with the INCOME panel (give it 10 seconds).
3. On your phone: drag a building from the bar; tap to place; swipe the bar sideways; run the first lessons of the training shift.
4. Open the speaker button and move the sliders.
