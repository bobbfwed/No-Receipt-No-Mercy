## What was done

### The Car Park is in the game as a fifth store design, in all eight sizes
- Rows of parked cars are solid, so they are free walls. Each row has ways through: a zebra-striped walkway straight across at the height of the first entrance, and others staggered from row to row. Empty bays on one side can be built in. The lanes between the rows hold the factory's marked floor.
- Drawn pieces: six kinds of car with rare extras (parking ticket, wheel clamp, up on bricks, steamed-up windows, a police car), a sports car parked across two bays with an insult keyed into the bonnet, trolley shelters, kerbed islands with lamp posts and pay machines, abandoned trolleys, coned-off potholes, and a burger van on the Superstore size and up. Tarmac ground, a hedge and fence round the outside, and its own colours on the small map.
- Pictures: [the real game, first sales event](img/2026-11-17-car-park-game.png), [close up](img/2026-11-17-car-park-closeup.png), and [the whole two-player size](../../docs/img/map-carpark.png) (also in the README).

### It has tills on two walls
- The usual bank in the right-hand wall and a second in the bottom wall. A solo player's entrance is the same distance from each (35 tiles and 35 on the one-player size). Shoppers walk to the nearest till, so lengthening one route sends the crowd to the other.
- The refund flash now lights only the till the shopper reached, and both banks have floor hatching and wall signs.
- DEB has 17 opening lines of her own for the Car Park and 6 for a refused building.

### The README opens with one play link
- It now starts "**Play version 0.2.08 now: (link)**". The second link under it is gone. The line is written by `tools/readme.mjs`, so it follows every version bump.

## Ideas for new shoppers (nothing built; approve, change or bin each one)

Each has one trick nobody in the game has yet, what beats it, and a super version. Size is how much work it is: S is a few numbers and a drawing, M needs a new rule, L touches several parts.

1. **Shoplifter.** Does not head for the tills. He walks to the nearest belt carrying finished goods, pockets one, and only then goes for a refund, which is worth what he took (a Gift Set is a $16 loss on the belt and a bigger refund at the till). Stopped on the way out, he drops it back on the belt.
   - Beat him with: security where the factory is, not just at the gaps.
   - Super: **Organised Retail Crime**. A holdall: takes three items.
   - Why: the first shopper who cares what the factory is making. Size M.
2. **Health and Safety Inspector.** Walks to the nearest working machine, tapes it off for 20 seconds, then the next one, and never goes near a till while a machine is running. He costs income, not refunds.
   - Beat him with: towers covering the machines. A crate wall does nothing: he has a lanyard.
   - Super: **Fire Marshal**. Tapes off everything within two tiles, security included.
   - Why: a threat aimed at the factory expert, where the Gym Bro is aimed at the maze. Size M.
3. **Hen Party.** Six women in sashes on one rope, with one shared patience bar. They walk as a conga line and leave together when the bar is empty; if they get through, that is six refunds.
   - Beat them with: anything that hits several at once. A Barcode Laser down the line counts six times.
   - Super: **Stag Do**. Ten of them, and the one in the mankini stops Exhibitionist-style: your forklift drivers stare.
   - Why: rewards area damage and laser placement the way nothing else does. Size M.
4. **Sovereign Citizen.** "I do not consent." The first three hits on him do nothing, and the count resets if nothing hits him for five seconds. He films everything on his phone.
   - Beat him with: sprinklers, which hit fast and strip it. Forklifts waste their big slow hits.
   - Super: **Sovereign Citizen With a Lawyer**. Six hits, and the TV Salesman cannot stop him (he does not contract with corporations).
   - Why: the first shopper where which tower you built matters more than how much. Size S.
5. **Burrito Guy.** Leaves a cloud behind him for a few seconds. Shoppers walking through it hurry (bad) and lose patience every second they are in it (good). Security inside the cloud fires slower, because the driver is gagging.
   - Beat him with: space. Keep him away from your best gap, or let him walk in front of a crowd on a long route.
   - Super: **Chili Cook-Off Champion**. Bigger cloud, and it lingers.
   - Why: helps and hurts at once, like the Teenager, but the choice is where to let him walk. Size M.
6. **Sample Hoarder.** Goes to every Free Samples stand on his way and eats it empty: the stand is out of action for 15 seconds and he gets patience back. Also sits down in front of the TV Salesman and will not move, so nobody else can watch.
   - Beat him with: damage before the first stand. Do not chain your stalls in one place.
   - Super: **Buffet Destroyer**. Empties a stand for the rest of the sales event.
   - Why: the counter to building nothing but stalls. Size S to M.
7. **Old Lady Paying in Pennies.** Very slow, very patient, and nobody can get past her in a gap one tile wide. The crowd piles up behind her (in your spray, if you planned it). At the till she takes 15 seconds to count her coins, and that till is shut to everyone else while she does.
   - Beat her with: nothing quickly. Use her: she is a cork. Give the crowd no second way round.
   - Super: **Two Old Ladies Having a Chat**. They stop dead for a natter in every doorway.
   - Why: a shopper you want in the right place rather than gone. Size M (shoppers do not block each other today).
8. **Divorcing Couple.** Two shoppers who arrive together. When one storms out, the other gets all his or her patience back and speeds up ("I am keeping the refund"), unless both go within three seconds of each other.
   - Beat them with: even damage on both, or one big gap that finishes them together.
   - Super: **Divorcing Couple With Lawyers**. Four of them, same rule.
   - Why: timing matters, which no shopper asks for today. Size S to M.

My pick if you want three to start: **Shoplifter**, **Hen Party** and **Sovereign Citizen**. They touch three different parts (the factory, area damage, tower choice) and two of them are small.

## Ideas for new bosses (nothing built)

Today there is one boss, the manager, with four titles. He shrugs off everything that slows and heals the crowd, so the answer to him is raw damage. Each idea below asks for a different answer.

1. **The Man With a Receipt.** The only customer who is in the right. Security cannot hurt him at all. His patience runs down by itself, and twice as fast whenever he is standing still: at free samples, at the TV Salesman, slipped on a wet floor, stuck behind a drunk. The longer and more annoying the walk, the sooner he gives up.
   - The answer: the maze and the stalls. The exact opposite of the manager.
2. **Fire Marshal.** Walks the route with a clipboard. Every building he passes within two tiles is red-tagged: switched off for the rest of the sales event. He does not heal anyone; he just takes your store apart from the entrance inward.
   - The answer: stop him early, and do not put everything in one place.
3. **The Coach Party.** A tour bus pulls up: forty pensioners, one patience bar between them. Each one who reaches a till is a refund; empty the bar and they all go back to the bus.
   - The answer: area damage and a long route. A boss that is a crowd.
4. **The Bailiff.** Fast for a boss. If he reaches a till he takes no refund: he repossesses each player's most expensive building, which is gone, with no rubble to rebuild.
   - The answer: whatever it takes. The stakes are your best tower, not the refund count.
5. **The Auditor.** While he is in the store, the kind of security the team has spent the most on works at half speed ("I have concerns about the forklift budget").
   - The answer: a mixed defence. He punishes building forty of one thing.
6. **Mystery Shopper.** Walks in looking like a Bargain Hunter, with no boss bar. He is revealed when he has lost a quarter of his patience or is halfway to the tills. Get through and Head Office "has findings": everything costs 10% more next sales event.
   - The answer: defence that hurts everyone early, so he shows himself in time.

**How they would take turns** (your call): I would keep a boss every fifth sales event and rotate the kinds in a fixed order, with the boss bar naming the next one during the build pause so players can prepare. The alternatives are a random boss each time, or the host choosing which bosses are in the game.

My pick if you want two to start: **The Man With a Receipt** (the opposite test to the manager, and it fits the game's name) and **The Coach Party**.

## How to check it
1. Open the published game once the version on the menu reads 0.2.08, choose **Car Park** under The store, and press Play solo. Or locally: `node tools/serve.mjs`, then http://localhost:5173/?solo=1&map=carpark&size=s
2. Put crates in front of all the bottom tills: the last one is refused, and DEB says why.
3. Block the zebra walkway with a machine and watch the crowd swing to the bottom tills.
4. `node --test` runs all the tests.
