## What was built, most important first

### 1. Five cards a game (your biggest item) is in, with the deal, the slots, the panel and an Off switch
- When everyone is ready, five trading cards fly in one at a time (bad, good, bad, good, odd), each large enough to read, then drop into five slots at the top that stay all game. Clicking or tapping the slots opens a panel with all five.
- Each of the three families has its own look, landing effect and sound, as you asked: red "SCREWED" cards land with a flash and a shake, green "LUCKY" cards with a burst of coins, purple "WEIRD" cards with a wobble.
- A card holds for 7 seconds (4.5 was too short to read on a phone), so the whole deal is 38 seconds. A Skip button ends it when every player has pressed it.
- The deck is 48 cards (17 bad, 20 good, 11 odd), and all 48 can be dealt. Names are in the tone you asked for ("Big Dick Energy", "Whiskey Dick", "Should Have Had Her Spayed", "Wrong Way, Dipshit"). The full list is in `PLAN.md`.
- "Cards: On / Off" is in the menu and the lobby (your later request). Off plays exactly as before.

### 2. ⚠️ The playtest: no dealt hand breaks the game; eight cards were retuned and two were reworked
- Over 5,000 scripted games with the new tool `tools/cardtest.mjs`: every card alone, twelve hands of five that between them hold every card, and forty hands dealt as a real game deals them.
- Your yardstick was "games end around event 16, losing anywhere from 12 to 19 is fine". Without cards the scripted players' median is 16.
- **Forty dealt hands, on the final rules with all 48 cards:** the plain scripted players' median is 16 (lowest 6, highest 18); 4 of 40 fall outside 12 to 19, all early collapses on seeds that are fragile anyway. A steadier scripted player (added for this test; it buys its next Press as soon as it can) never falls outside: lowest 13, median 17, highest 18.
- **Why a steadier player was needed:** the plain scripted players have no spare money in their first events. Starting with $25 less drops them from event 15.3 to 12.3. So any card that moves money early mostly shows up as "tipped a fragile game over", not as the card's own effect. Both players are reported everywhere.
- **Retuned (old to new):** Little Blue Pills +30% to +25% patience; Shrinkflation +10% to +8% prices; Fewer, Fatter +25% to +15% patience; Flash Mob -20% to -25% patience; Artisanal +30% to +20% sale price; Father's Day dads -35% to -28% patience; Payday Friday refunds +40% to +60%; Double Dipping one shopper in three to one in five.
- **Your own numbers held up and are unchanged:** 3x Mystery Boxes, 1.7x Gadgets, 1.5x Gift Sets, bosses with 20% more patience, cosplayers at 0.8 tiles a second.
- **Two cards did the opposite of their side and were reworked** (your decision at the end of the run):
  - **Free Samples: Now 80 Proof** (good): a shopper who turned into a Day Drinker used to gain patience. Now he never gains any, hits the floor on the spot, and loses 40% of the patience he had left. With the scripted player that builds sample tables, the steady version lasts 0.6 to 0.8 events longer.
  - **Five-Finger Discount** (bad): the longer reach kept shoplifters under fire, which helped you. Now shoplifters go for goods twice as often and every theft gives the thief back 40% of his patience (a pink "YOINK!" shows). The plain scripted players lose about half an event sooner; the steady ones barely move.
- **A real flaw found and fixed:** the cards that pick "a share of the shoppers" (Double Dipping, Off the Leash, Pre-Workout, Should Have Had Her Spayed, Eyes Up Here Dad, Emotional Support Animal, Wrong Way Dipshit) picked the very same shoppers every game, because shoppers are numbered in the same order every game. The pick now changes with each game. That fix is what exposed Double Dipping as too strong.
- **Cards only real games can judge:** Eyes Up Here, Dad (it never triggers in the scripted layout), Emotional Support Animal, anything involving the Barcode Laser, and the cards about babies, puppies, gym bros' hits, dog pee and bosses, which barely move the scripted result even at extreme numbers.

## What was added
1. **Pause.** A button just before 1x 2x 3x 5x (two bars; an orange play triangle while paused), or the `P` key. Any player can pause and anyone can resume. Everything freezes for everyone: shoppers, belts, the build countdown, the card deal. A PAUSED sign over the floor names who paused; clicking it resumes. The music drops to 30%. DEB mocks whoever paused. A game joined or loaded while paused lands paused. Pictures: [the sign during a sales event](img/0.3-pause-banner-wave.png), [phone](img/0.3-pause-phone-paused.png), [the button, hosted](img/0.3-pause-button-hosted.png).
2. **A selected building keeps its reach on the floor.** Click a security building and its current reach stays drawn (a white ring, or the Barcode Laser's beam strip) for as long as its panel is open. Pointing at the Range upgrade adds the reach after that upgrade as a dashed gold outline; Damage, Speed and Durability change nothing on the floor. Pictures: [pointing at Range](img/0.3-range-sprinkler-range-hover.png), [pointing at Damage](img/0.3-range-sprinkler-damage-hover.png), [Barcode Laser](img/0.3-range-laser-range-hover.png).
   - **On a phone** the reach shows on a tap as it did in 0.2.x, now in the clearer white style, and grows when Range is bought. Pictures: [before](img/0.3-range-phone-before.png), [tapped](img/0.3-range-phone-selected.png), [after buying Range](img/0.3-range-phone-upgraded.png).
   - Not asked for but needed: when the building's reach would be hidden under its own panel, the panel now opens at the top of the floor instead ([phone](img/0.3-range-phone-low.png)).
   - Limit: a Range button you cannot afford is disabled, and the browser may not report the mouse over it, so the dashed preview may not show then.
3. **The hand of cards moved** from the top middle of the floor to directly under the ON SHIFT panel, same width, following it down as players join; beside the panel when the panel is tall. Phones unchanged.
