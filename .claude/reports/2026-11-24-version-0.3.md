## What was built, most important first

### 1. Five cards a game (your biggest item) is in, with the deal, the slots, the panel and an Off switch
- When everyone is ready, five trading cards fly in one at a time (bad, good, bad, good, odd), each large enough to read, then drop into five slots at the top that stay all game. Clicking or tapping the slots opens a panel with all five.
- Each of the three families has its own look, landing effect and sound, as you asked: red "SCREWED" cards land with a flash and a shake, green "LUCKY" cards with a burst of coins, purple "WEIRD" cards with a wobble.
- A card holds for 7 seconds (4.5 was too short to read on a phone), so the whole deal is 38 seconds. A Skip button ends it when every player has pressed it.
- The deck is 48 cards (17 bad, 20 good, 11 odd); 46 can be dealt. Names are in the tone you asked for ("Big Dick Energy", "Whiskey Dick", "Should Have Had Her Spayed", "Wrong Way, Dipshit"). The full list is in `PLAN.md`.
- "Cards: On / Off" is in the menu and the lobby (your later request). Off plays exactly as before.

### 2. ⚠️ The playtest: no dealt hand breaks the game, eight cards were retuned, two were held back
- Over 5,000 scripted games with the new tool `tools/cardtest.mjs`: every card alone, twelve hands of five that between them hold every card, and forty hands dealt as a real game deals them.
- Your yardstick was "games end around event 16, losing anywhere from 12 to 19 is fine". Without cards the scripted players' median is 16.
- **Forty dealt hands, on the final rules:** the plain scripted players' median is 16 (lowest 6, highest 19); 3 of 40 fall outside 12 to 19, all early collapses on seeds that are fragile anyway. A steadier scripted player (added for this test; it buys its next Press as soon as it can) never falls outside: lowest 13, median 17, highest 19.
- **Why a steadier player was needed:** the plain scripted players have no spare money in their first events. Starting with $25 less drops them from event 15.3 to 12.3. So any card that moves money early mostly shows up as "tipped a fragile game over", not as the card's own effect. Both players are reported everywhere.
- **Retuned (old to new):** Little Blue Pills +30% to +25% patience; Shrinkflation +10% to +8% prices; Fewer, Fatter +25% to +15% patience; Flash Mob -20% to -25% patience; Artisanal +30% to +20% sale price; Father's Day dads -35% to -28% patience; Payday Friday refunds +40% to +60%; Double Dipping one shopper in three to one in five.
- **Your own numbers held up and are unchanged:** 3x Mystery Boxes, 1.7x Gadgets, 1.5x Gift Sets, bosses with 20% more patience, cosplayers at 0.8 tiles a second.
- **A real flaw found and fixed:** the cards that pick "a share of the shoppers" (Double Dipping, Off the Leash, Pre-Workout, Should Have Had Her Spayed, Eyes Up Here Dad, Emotional Support Animal, Wrong Way Dipshit) picked the very same shoppers every game, because shoppers are numbered in the same order every game. The pick now changes with each game. That fix is what exposed Double Dipping as too strong.
- **Cards only real games can judge:** Eyes Up Here, Dad (it never triggers in the scripted layout), Emotional Support Animal, anything involving the Barcode Laser, and the cards about babies, puppies, gym bros' hits, dog pee and bosses, which barely move the scripted result even at extreme numbers.

## How to verify
1. `node --test` (about 8 seconds).
2. `node tools/serve.mjs`, open `http://localhost:5173/`, start a game, press Ready and watch the deal.
3. `http://localhost:5173/?solo=1&cards=bde,lootbox,spayed,supportdog,fathersday` forces a hand to look at particular cards.
4. On your phone: pick a security building, drag a finger across the floor, tap Rotate with another finger, then tap the floor with another finger to cancel.
5. `node tools/cardtest.mjs deal` repeats the dealt-hands playtest (about 6 minutes).
