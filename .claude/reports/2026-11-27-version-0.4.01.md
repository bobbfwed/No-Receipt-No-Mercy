Built 2026-11-27 from the owner's list of nine fixes and eight more sent while the work was under way. Committed on `main` locally. **Not pushed** (the owner pushes), not tagged, and not yet played by a person.

## What was done, most important first

### Belts are laid as a line: press, drag, release
- The route is the shortest with the fewest turns, usually a straight line or an L. It goes round pillars and machines and stops where your cash runs out. While you drag, the planned belts are drawn with one price label. A single click still places one belt.
- A line that crosses a straight belt turns that belt into an Overpass, so the two do not mix.
- Pictures: [the plan while dragging](img/0.4.01/final-02a-belt-plan.png), [a crossing made into an Overpass](img/0.4.01/final-02d-cross-placed.png), [on a phone](img/0.4.01/final-13b-phone-belt-plan.png).

### A part placed on a belt replaces the belt
- Splitter, Overpass, Unpaid Intern, machines, Rebrander and Clearance Bin go straight onto a plain belt. You pay the part's price less what the belt cost; the label reads "SWAPS THE BELT: $13". [Picture](img/0.4.01/final-03-splitter-swap.png).

### The Returns Drop has no direction
- It shares junk evenly between everything touching it that takes junk, and passes over a side that is backed up, as a Splitter does. Its total rate is unchanged. It is redrawn as a chute with an outlet on each side. [Picture](img/0.4.01/draw-drop-4.png).

### Duct Tape and the Tape Winder are gone; every security building is reinforced for cash
- Eight levels, one more gym bro hit per sales event each. The first level costs one eighth of the building's price (at least $10), each level 1.6 times the last: Pepper Sprinkler and Wet Floor Sign from $10, Free Samples $15, Forklift Turret $25, TV Salesman $56, Barcode Laser $88.
- REINFORCE is on every building's sheet and the Reinforce tool (key U) works on all of them. A small plate on the building shows its level. Pictures: [sheet](img/0.4.01/final-06a-sprinkler-sheet.png), [plates on a phone](img/0.4.01/input-reinforce-plates-844.png).

### Wet Floor Signs take nothing from The Back
- Four cash upgrades from an UPGRADE button on the sign's sheet, shown as four pips over the sign. [Picture](img/0.4.01/final-06d-wetfloor-upgraded.png).

### Razor Wire turns ordinary shoppers too, and is easier to see through
- A wire direction is refused if it would cut shoppers off, and placing a building counts all planned wire as a wall, so wire arriving later can never seal the store. Shopper routes are drawn round live wire: [picture](img/0.4.01/input-route-round-wire.png).
- The wire is drawn as thin coils between two fine lines. [Before](img/0.4.01/draw-wire-before-pc.png), [after](img/0.4.01/draw-wire-after-pc.png).
- A pulsing red lamp sits on the Dumbwaiter of a building that has a wire direction and no wire. [Picture](img/0.4.01/draw-lamp-close.png).

### Cards
- Each card shows half a second less (6.5 s). Clicking the card on show brings the next one when every connected player has clicked, never sooner than a second after it appeared. The screen says who it is waiting for. The "skip all" button is unchanged. Pictures: [the hint](img/0.4.01/final-09c-deal.png), [waiting for the other player](img/0.4.01/final-15a-twoplayer-steve-waiting.png).

### Smaller things
- **Clearance Bin pays double**: $0.20, $0.50, $1, $2 by brand tier.
- **Phone**: the view travels past the map's edges far enough to pull the top and bottom rows clear of the interface, with black beyond. [Top](img/0.4.01/final-12d-zoomed-top.png), [bottom](img/0.4.01/final-12e-zoomed-bottom.png).
- **Q** puts down whatever you hold or have selected: a tool from the bar, a selected building's sheet, or a belt line in mid-drag.
- **The Unpaid Intern is animated**: he lifts the item off the belt and swings it over, stands on his phone when idle, and stands holding the item when the Dumbwaiter is full. [Picture](img/0.4.01/draw-intern-sides-1150.png).
- **Items slide through a Splitter** instead of vanishing and reappearing, and slide out of the Returns Drop and machines the same way. [Frames](img/0.4.01/draw-split-strip.png). Limit: the rules move an item across a Splitter in a tick or two, so the drawing catches up at about 1.5 times belt speed. Exact belt speed would need a rules change. A Splitter handing straight into an Overpass still pops; it is rare.
- The training shift, the Handbook, DEB's lines and the README follow all of the above. The Splitter and Rebrander lessons now place the part straight onto the belt.

### ⚠️ The independent review found a serious bug in this round's work, fixed before release
- With wire now a wall to shoppers, a player could shut a shopper in for good by building crates on tiles that carried planned but unstocked wire, and the sales event never ended. Fixed, with the case as a test.
- Three smaller ones, also fixed with tests: an Overpass made by a line could reverse a belt that had nothing feeding it; a line cut short by cash could end pointing into the side of the belt it meant to cross; the host and joined-player checksum did not cover what was spent on a part.

## Test results
- `node --test` (my run on the final code): **728 tests, 728 pass, 0 fail**.
- `node tools/layout.mjs` (my run): **PASS 36 of 36**, including new checks that the edge rows can be pulled clear on a phone.
- Independent review of the rules changes: 16 whole games on all seven store designs, 400,000 ticks, 155,272 random and malformed commands, 4,322 belt lines and 3,678 belt replacements. No crash, no free money, no lost or duplicated items, no difference between host and joined player. It needed a hand-built case to find the sealed shopper.
- Host against joined player after the fixes (`tools/perf/desync.mjs`): 6 games, 65,987 full comparisons, 0 differences.
- Worst-case cost on the largest store: one belt line command 8.6 ms, one placement check 1.6 ms.
- Browser check of the real game, 15 checks at PC size and at 844x390 with touch: no JavaScript errors. 13 passed outright. Two were only partly confirmed: the animations (still pictures only), and the two-player deal, where one player's screen was a card ahead of the other's while one tab was in the background. The "both must click" rule itself is covered by tests; cards also still move on by themselves after 6.5 s.
