## What was done

### The Gym Bro has a limit
- **Gym Bro:** 2 hits per visit, 10 seconds of rest after each. **Roid Rager:** 3 hits, 5 seconds.
- **Every hit counts,** as you chose: one that a Durability level absorbs uses up a hit and starts the rest. One Durability level on a crate soaks up a hit.
- **While resting, and once his hits are gone, he walks round crates like anyone else,** as you chose, and hits nothing. With a hit ready he goes straight through crates as before.
- Unchanged: how often he tries to hit something that is not a crate (every 1.5 seconds with a 15% chance; the Roid Rager every 0.9 seconds with a 40% chance).
- The handbook, the help text, the Gym Bros lesson, the README and DEB's two introductions say the new rule. Three of DEB's jokes ("Men like that never go around anything") are now only loosely true and were left as jokes.
- **Effect in the scripted games:** on one seed, pieces wrecked over a whole game went from 73 to 10, 116 to 26 and 108 to 25 for the three scripted players.
